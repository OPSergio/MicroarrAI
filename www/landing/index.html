Peptide microarray analysis pipeline

From raw arrays to
candidate biomarkers

MicroarrAI processes peptide microarray data, from raw signal to candidate biomarkers, and maps the results onto the 3D structure of the antigen. The sections below describe each stage of the pipeline.

01 — Preprocessing

Background correction and normalization

Arrays are background-corrected spot by spot, non-specific signal is removed using negative controls, and the matrix is normalized — z-score, median scaling or quantile. Zero-variance features are dropped automatically.

At this stage the 3D structure is not colored by data.

02 — Differential analysis

Identifying differentially expressed peptides

Parametric and non-parametric tests, GLM/LM models, AUC, BH-adjusted p-values and fold-change are used to identify the peptides that differ between conditions.

The 3D structure is now colored by expression level at each residue.

03 — Machine learning

Ranking candidate biomarkers

Random Forest, C5.0 and SVM-RFE are used to rank peptides by importance and select candidate biomarkers.

Residues corresponding to the selected biomarkers are highlighted in orange on the structure.

04 — 3D visualization

Mapping biomarkers onto the structure

Candidate peptides are mapped onto the AlphaFold structure to show their position, surface accessibility and potential interaction with immune receptors.
