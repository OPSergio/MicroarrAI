    Before you start

    Prepare your files

    MicroarrAI reads what your scanner already exports. Most problems later on come from these three files, so five minutes here saves an afternoon.

    Channel convention. On two-channel slides MicroarrAI assumes Channel 1, red (635 nm) = IgG4 and Channel 2, green (532 nm) = IgE. If your slides use other antibodies (e.g. IgG and IgA), you rename the channels in Step 2; nothing in the files needs to change.
    Optional

    Practise first with an example cohort

    Three synthetic two-group studies ship with the app, written exactly like a ScanArray export. Load one from Preprocessing › ☰ sidebar › Example data, or download its tables to see the expected layout.
