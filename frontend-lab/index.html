01 — Preprocessing

Clean signal, honest baseline

Arrays are background-corrected spot by spot, non-specific signal is removed using negative controls, and the matrix is normalized — z-score, median scaling or quantile. Zero-variance features are dropped automatically.

The protein stays neutral. No conclusions drawn yet.

02 — Differential analysis

Where the signal lives

Parametric and non-parametric tests, GLM/LM models, AUC, BH-adjusted p-values and fold-change surface the peptides that truly separate conditions.

Watch the structure light up — expression is now painted onto the residues.

03 — Machine learning

The biomarkers emerge

Random Forest, C5.0 and SVM-RFE rank the features that matter. The most informative peptides rise to the surface as candidate biomarkers.

The biomarker residues now stand out on the protein in orange.

04 — 3D visualization

Read them in context

Candidate peptides are mapped onto the AlphaFold structure so you can judge accessibility and immune-receptor interaction in space — not just on a list.
